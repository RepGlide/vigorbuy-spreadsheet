Review Source Guide

Tell publisher statements, third-party guides, claimed reviews, search titles, and visible evidence apart.

Read the source, then the claim

A review means something different depending on who wrote it. Note the publisher before you weigh the words.

Publisher statement

The seller or platform speaking about itself. It is promotion, not an independent view.

Third-party guide

Someone outside the seller writes a guide. Its independence is a claim too — check what it discloses.

Claimed first-hand review

A statement of personal experience. It cannot be verified from the page alone; note that it is a claim.

Search title

A headline written to rank in search. It is a label, not evidence.

Visible evidence

What the page actually shows — screenshots, dates, or data. Only this can be checked directly.

?Open item: is the publisher identified, or is the claim floating without a source?

A worked read

  1. Source: a page with no publisher shown.
  2. Claim: it calls itself “honest”.
  3. Result: the publisher stays Not stated.
