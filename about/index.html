About This Resource

VigorBuy Spreadsheet is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a shared VigorBuy spreadsheet and want to read review claims carefully.

What this resource is

  • A directory that links to twelve product categories for further browsing.
  • A set of guides for reading review sources, pros and cons, and comparisons.
  • A reminder that a claim needs a publisher and evidence.

What this resource is not

  • Not the official VigorBuy website, and not affiliated with or endorsed by VigorBuy or Google.
  • Not a review platform, comparison site, product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, or order tracker.
  • Not a source of verified, trusted, or true-false scores for any product or claim.

Independence

This resource describes how to check who published a claim, what type of claim it is, what evidence is shown, and when the context applies. It does not review products, rate sellers, or verify claims.
