Review / Guide / Promotion

The Type Changes What It Can Claim

1What the type decides

  • A review claims an evaluation.
  • A guide claims instruction.
  • A promotion claims a reason to act.

2What the type does not decide

  • That the writer is independent.
  • That the writer has first-hand experience.
  • That the claim is current or complete.
The Empty Quote Frame

A Quote With No Source Is Just a Frame

Without a publisher and evidence, a quote is an empty frame — a shape waiting for content.

1What to record

  • The publisher, if it is shown.
  • The claim type, if it is clear.
  • The evidence, if any is visible.

2What to mark

  • Not stated when a publisher is missing.
  • Not stated when the type is unclear.
  • Not stated when no evidence is shown.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a VigorBuy spreadsheet?

A VigorBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to put in context, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official VigorBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by VigorBuy or Google.

Does the review-source check verify or rate anything?

No. The review-source check is a reading framework. It is not a review platform, comparison site, product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, or order tracker. It does not produce verified, trusted, or true-false scores.

What should I check about a review claim?

Who published it, what type of claim it is, what evidence the page actually shows, and what date, scope, or situation the conclusion applies to.

Do pros and cons prove anything by themselves?

No. Pros and cons only mean something with a clear scope, time, usage situation, and evidence. Without those, they are opinions, not conclusions.

Where do I place an order?

After you have checked the source of each review claim, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
